Mi Dash Cam tips

This help applies to the European Mi Dash Cam app and the original Mi Dash Cam, model MJXCJLY01BY.

Connect the phone

  1. Power on the dash cam and enable its Wi-Fi.
  2. On the phone, allow the app's requested location/Wi-Fi permission. Android uses this permission when an app identifies nearby Wi-Fi networks.
  3. If automatic connection does not work, open the phone's Wi-Fi settings and join the dash cam network manually. Accept the network even though Android reports that it has no internet.
  4. Return to Mi Dash Cam and tap +. Keep the phone connected to the dash cam Wi-Fi while viewing or transferring recordings.
The dash cam Wi-Fi is a direct local connection. It is normal for that network to have no internet access.

View and save recordings

  • Open the connected camera to see the live preview.
  • Open the recording browser to view normal or emergency clips.
  • Use Export to copy selected clips to DCIM/Mi Dash Cam on the phone.

Recording card

Use an 8–64 GB microSD/TF card rated Class 10 or faster. The camera is not supplied with a card.
