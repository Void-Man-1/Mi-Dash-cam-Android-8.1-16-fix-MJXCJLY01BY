Installation

For Mi Dash Cam model MJXCJLY01BY.

Four-step installation

  1. Clean the inside of the windscreen and attach the electrostatic sticker, preferably near the rear-view mirror and horizontally centred for a wide view.
  2. Connect the dash cam to its mounting bracket.
  3. Attach the bracket to the electrostatic sticker using the heat-resistant adhesive pad.
  4. Route the power cable safely and connect the supplied car charger to a 5 V / 2 A cigarette-lighter outlet.
Driving safety: do not obstruct the driver's view, interfere with airbags, or leave loose cable near vehicle controls. Confirm that the camera view is level before driving.

Vehicle cautions

  • Xiaomi does not recommend this mounting method where the windscreen angle to the ground is below 63°, including some trucks, buses, and vans.
  • Some vehicles keep the accessory socket powered after the engine is switched off. In that case, unplug the camera after parking to avoid draining the vehicle battery.
  • Insert a Class 10 or faster 8–64 GB microSD/TF card before recording.

Source: Xiaomi's official installation and specifications pages for MJXCJLY01BY. Always follow local road and vehicle-safety requirements.
