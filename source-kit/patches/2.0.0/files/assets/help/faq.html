Frequently asked questions

Why does the camera Wi-Fi say “No internet”?

The phone connects directly to the dash cam. That local Wi-Fi is only for camera control and video transfer, so no internet access is expected. Stay connected and return to the app.

The app cannot find or connect to the camera

  • Confirm the camera is powered on and its Wi-Fi is enabled.
  • Allow the app's requested location/Wi-Fi permission.
  • Join the camera network manually in Android Wi-Fi settings, accept the no-internet warning, then return to Mi Dash Cam.
  • Temporarily prevent the phone from switching to mobile data or another saved Wi-Fi network while testing.
  • This app is for the original Mi Dash Cam MJXCJLY01BY; newer 1S or Dash Cam 2 models use different software.

Where are exported videos stored?

In DCIM/Mi Dash Cam. Android should also index a completed export so it appears in compatible gallery apps.

Playback stops or the recording list is empty

Keep the phone on the camera Wi-Fi, confirm the supported microSD card is inserted and readable, and retry close to the camera. Camera recordings are streamed over the local link; disconnecting that Wi-Fi interrupts playback.

What card should I use?

An 8–64 GB microSD/TF card rated Class 10 or faster. Xiaomi states that no card is included.

Why did the old help screen show 502 Bad Gateway?

The original European help endpoints returned 502 during testing and their content could not be recovered. This patched build uses bundled offline help for the four home-screen help items and the Wi-Fi connection guide.

Product facts are from Xiaomi's official MJXCJLY01BY pages. Android troubleshooting is based on the app's recovered connection logic and compatibility testing.
